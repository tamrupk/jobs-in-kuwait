Most In-Demand Jobs in Kuwait

The jobs with the highest number of daily openings in Kuwait right now are:

  • Housemaids – live-in and part-time domestic workers for families
  • Drivers – family drivers, company drivers and taxi drivers
  • Delivery riders – food and parcel delivery across Kuwait City
  • Helpers & cleaners – office, restaurant and household helpers
  • Sales staff & accountants – retail shops, showrooms and offices

Housemaid Jobs in Kuwait

Housemaid jobs are the most posted category in Kuwait. Families in Salmiya, Hawally, Farwaniya, Mahboula and Fahaheel hire housemaids every week. Duties usually include cooking, cleaning, laundry, ironing and childcare. Live-in housemaids commonly work from early morning to evening with one weekly day off, while part-time housemaids work fixed daily hours.

Driver Jobs in Kuwait

Driver jobs in Kuwait cover family drivers, company drivers, delivery drivers and heavy-vehicle drivers. Employers usually require a valid Kuwait driving licence. Family driver roles often include accommodation, while delivery rider jobs pay per order on top of a basic salary.

Tips for Job Seekers in Kuwait

  • Never pay any fee to get a job – genuine employers do not ask job seekers for money.
  • Meet employers in public places for the first interview when possible.
  • Keep copies of your civil ID, passport and visa documents ready.
  • Check new ads daily – the best openings fill within days.
